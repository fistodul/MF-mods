## 10. Maps & the Editor (continued)

### E. Zones, Fog, Sky & Ceiling
- A `ZoneInfo` in a zone replaces the `LevelInfo`'s zone settings for that zone entirely. Copy what the base map set in its `LevelInfo` (fog, ambient) into the new `ZoneInfo`.
- **The renderer stops drawing at `DistanceFogEnd`, even with `bDistanceFog=False`.** `ZoneInfo`'s default of 8000 makes distant land vanish. A short fog end in a zone you can see out of (through a door or window) leaves hall-of-mirrors smearing beyond it. Keep the end longer than any line of sight (corner to corner of the world box).
- Zone portals: a sheet brush or volume brush with `PolyFlags=603979785` and polygon `Flags=603980041`. Water volumes: `PolyFlags=603979788` with a translucent water texture, plus a `RageWaterZone` inside. `ZoneVelocity` on a water zone makes a current; Z+1800 lifts a swimming player at a good pace.
- Don't wrap open-air areas in invisible zone-portal volumes. They caused hall-of-mirrors smearing around them. Traces also stop at non-solid portal faces, so the Battle Royale mod (10F) dropped items on them and spawned players inside geometry.
- The sky is any surface with `Flags=128` (FakeBackdrop), drawn from the `SkyZoneInfo` room. To lower a ceiling, lower the world subtract brush itself (10D). An extra solid slab is a real surface that spawning traces hit.
- Fall damage (`Pawn.TakeFallingDamage`): `100 * ((V + 750) / 450)^2` for landing speed `V` past 750 (dead at 1200). `ZoneTerminalVelocity` caps falling speed per zone (default 2500): 740 lands without damage, 830 costs 3 HP. Zone gravity alone doesn't save a long fall.
- Swimming: `WaterSpeed` 200 and `UnderWaterTime` 20 s, then 5 damage every 2 s. That's 4000 units of swimming per breath at best.
- Terrain meshes are one-sided heightfields, and an island's mesh usually sits on a flat seabed plate. Moved or scaled up the water column, that plate floats as a visible sheet. Hide it under the sea floor, or cover it with BSP from just above it (32 units, against z-fighting) down into the floor. `BR-thebigisland`'s mini island sits on a round reef (`prism_polys` frustum).
- Ladders (stock pattern, used by ~50 maps): a hidden `LadderClimber` (`CollisionHeight` = half the climb, `ClimbSpeed=0.5`, `Rotation` facing the wall) about 45 units off a vertical wall, with `Ladder` navigation points just below its bottom and above its top. A touching player switches to `PHYS_Ladder`. The visible ladder is a static mesh, e.g. `mf-Ghetto`'s `Ladder` (1040 tall, 20 off the wall; copy it with `import_actor`). Sloped terrain needs a vertical BSP pillar behind the ladder.
- Vehicles in water: vehicle physics only knows one global water height, set by a `PhysicalWaterInfo` (`WaterHeight` = the surface Z, like mf-waterfront's). Without one, `RagePlayer.PreBeginPlay` leaves it at -100000, so vehicles find no water surface in water zones. Boats come from a `VehicleGenerator` with `VehicleClass=Class'boat.boat'` (`OBJ LOAD FILE=..\System\boat.u` first); the map then needs `boat.u` and `Physics/boat.COL` on clients. Boarding from the water is unreliable (vehicles 11G), so moor boats beside a jetty (`BR-thebigisland`: a plank deck at sea level +24 with the boat 200 off its centre line). Its jetty brushes are semisolid (`PolyFlags=32`): built solid, after `MAP REBUILD` every team `PlayerStart` failed (`Couldn't spawn player at ...`, a client crash joining a team game), cause unknown. Vehicles still collide with semisolid brushes (a buggy dropped on a deck rested 37 above it, as on the ground). A failed spawn logs `Destroying car on creation` and retries every 5 s; a level holds only 64 physical objects (vehicles 11G).
- Ambient sound: `AmbientSound` loops, `PeriodicSound` plays for `Period` seconds every `Period + Interval` (offset by `PhaseShift`). Find stock examples with `upkg.py refs levelSFX` (stock, so no download) and copy their settings, e.g. mf-waterfront's `LappingWater01` (radius 96, volume 128, ~4000 apart) and `Seagulls1` (80/160, period 16, interval 20), mf-Prehistoric's `CrashingWater` (90/100, 4/13). `BR-thebigisland` places them along the coast where terrain triangles cross the sea level.
- Physical props (`Ball`s) count toward the level's 64 physical objects, which vehicles share (vehicles 11G). A free-standing stack of them creeps upwards (40 apart, the top ball rose ~1000 units in 75 s); a ceiling one unit above the top ball holds it.
- Air pocket (verified): carve a chamber below the water and reach it by tunnels from the water zone. Put a horizontal sheet brush across the chamber at the water line (polygon `Flags=603989516` with a water texture, `PolyFlags=603979788`), and a `ZoneInfo` above it. The sheet seals off a dry zone where players surface and breathe. Add a ledge only a few units above the water, and light it so the way up can be seen from the tunnels.

### F. Battle Royale Maps (`battleRoyale`, map prefix `BR-`)
- Item scatter traces up from the map's `Sunlight` to the ceiling (`upperLimit`), then along ±X and ±Y at that height for the world limits. Items and non-air spawns trace straight down from the ceiling; air spawns drop 500 below it. Keep the `Sunlight` (`LightType=LT_None` if it shouldn't light) below the ceiling, and keep its four horizontal traces clear of geometry and zone portals.
- The safe zone centres on a `RageDominationTimer` and measures distance in 3D, so a centre high above the playing area leaves everyone outside. `battleRoyale.PostBeginPlay` walks the actor list until it has seen a `Sunlight` and a timer, keeping the last timer seen; Domination takes the first. `BR-thebigisland` lists the Holdout (on the platform), an island timer with `CondGameType=Class'Engine.GameInfo'` (irrelevant in every mode), then the `Sunlight`; imported actors keep their T3D order. The zone only exists with `enableSafeZone=True` in the server's config.
- Keep the safe-zone timer's default `CondGameType`: outside Domination it is then hidden and inert, while `CondGameType=None` logs `Accessed None` in its `PostBeginPlay`. Don't set `bHidden` or `bCollideActors=False` on it either: that leaves Domination (Holdout) with a point nobody can see or take.
- The `battleRoyale` package can't be rebuilt: its model and texture sources aren't in the repo.

### G. Game-Mode Actors
What each mode looks for, as laid out in stock mf-waterfront (copy its placement and heights over the floor):
- Team spawns: `PlayerStart` with `TeamNumber` 0 (blue) or 1 (red). Neutral spawns need `TeamNumber=255`: non-team games (`RageDeathMatch.FindPlayerStart`) only take `TeamNumber >= 2`, and a default (0) start is a blue spawn. ZombieGame, TugGame and KOH also tell bases from the rest by 255.
- Per team, one objective spot shared by `RageFlagBase` (CTF), `RageDetonationLock` (Detonation) and `RageSafe` (Safe Cracker), each with `Team`; plus `TrailerPark` and `TrailerDelivery` (`Team`, `CollisionRadius=128`), and team crates (`LoadoutCrate_Blue*`/`Red*` work in team games only, `LoadoutCrate_DM*` in the others; Battle Royale removes DM crates).
- Loadout at spawns without a walled `LoadoutZone`: a hidden team crate (`LoadoutCrate_Blue`, `bHidden=True`, `bCollideActors=False`) on each team `PlayerStart`. Its `LoadoutCrate` (radius 129) lets players spawn into the loadout screen and reopen it there. Don't place a bare `LoadoutCrate`: its `PreBeginPlay` skips the relevance check, so it exists in every mode, and the loadout key (`ServerTryLoadout`) doesn't check relevance either.
- Middle: one `RageDominationTimer` (Holdout; arenaMode and KOH reuse it), a `TrailerGenerator` (cab and trailer; MoBall spawns its ball there and its goals at the `TrailerPark`s, Racing its cars at the parks), and ~10 `RageDetPossibleKeyPos` (the Detonation key; ZombieGame and TugGame also spawn players there, KOH picks hills from them).
- `RoadPoint`s along drivable routes: vehicle bots path on them, and `trailerGameV2` moves the trailer generator to a random one within 10000 of it. `VehicleGenerator`s (so `TrailerGenerator` and `TruckGenerator`) and `TrailerPark`s are `RoadPoint`s too; the original `trailerGameV2.u` picked those as well, spawning the cab on a delivery zone or a boat spot. The rebuilt one (source exported into `trailerGameV2/Classes`) takes plain `RoadPoint`s only. Keep them 1000+ from each `TrailerDelivery`: the trailer spawns 244 behind the cab, facing either way, and one landing in a delivery zone delivers itself.
- The Trailer's trailer blows up the moment it is in a water zone (`BombTrailer.Tick`); other vehicles only get `bVehicleStuck`. Keep water zones off the trailer's route; `BR-thebigisland`'s waterfall foot is 1700 from it.
- The skirmish map list (`RageMenuMapSelector.AddMaps`) shows maps named with the chosen mode's `MapPrefix` (`BR-`, `CTF-`, `TRA-`, ...) plus every `MF-` map (`bCanBePlayedInMFMap`, true for all `RageDeathMatch` modes). A map meant for many modes needs the `MF-` prefix; the server URL loads any name.
- `mutPack.addWeaps` fills `LoadoutZone`s (team games) in `PostBeginPlay` and, since its rebuild, every `LoadoutCrate` on the first `Tick`. All crates share one set of always-relevant items (networking 2F); 31 per crate were ~800 replicated actors on this map. Mutators can't catch crates in `CheckReplacement`: `LoadoutCrate.PreBeginPlay` returns at its first empty slot before `Super`, and the decorations spawn their crate after their own relevance check (only `LoadoutCrate_DM` checks again, in `PostBeginPlay`, which the old build relied on: DM crates only).
- `CondGameType` must equal the game class exactly (`GameInfo.IsRelevant`), so subclasses don't inherit objectives. Navigation points can't be deleted, so mods still find them.
- New navigation points only join `Level.NavigationPointList`, which spawn code walks, after `PATHS DEFINE`.
- `arenaMode` used to spawn its timer at the game info (the world origin) and then move it to the Holdout, so it failed on maps whose origin is outside the world (`BR-thebigisland`, ceiling at Z -641). It now spawns it at the Holdout; servers and clients need that rebuilt `arenaMode.u`.

### H. Testing a Map on a Local Server
- Run `UCC.exe server <map>?game=<Pkg.Class>?mutator=<...> -forcelogflush` from the `System` short path and read its stdout. The `log=` file is written in 4 KB blocks, so killing the server loses its tail.
- For facts the log doesn't show (spawns chosen, vehicles and their positions, objectives' `bHidden`), compile a throwaway probe mutator that logs them from a `Timer`. Build it with a copy of the ini that adds it to `EditPackages` (`UCC.exe make ini=Test.ini`); the same copy can set `MinPlayers`. Delete the package, `.u` and ini afterwards.
